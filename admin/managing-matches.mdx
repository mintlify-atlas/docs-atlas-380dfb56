---
title: "Scheduling and updating match results"
description: "Create upcoming fixtures, record match results with scores, and update or delete matches from the Partidos tab in the FC Cañaveral admin dashboard."
---

The **Partidos** tab lets you build and maintain the full match calendar for FC Cañaveral. You can schedule future fixtures, record the final score once a match has been played, and correct or remove any entry at any time.

## The Partidos tab

Open the admin dashboard at `/admin` and click the **Partidos** tab. The table loads all matches from `GET /api/partidos`, ordered by date ascending, with each match showing the rival, date and time, location, and current scoreline.

Every row in the table is fully editable inline — you do not need a separate edit form.

## Scheduling a new fixture

Use the **Programar Nuevo Partido** form at the top of the tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter the match details">
    Fill in the following fields:

    - **Rival** — the name of the opposing team
    - **Campo / Ubicación** — the ground or venue where the match will be played
    - **Date and time** — use the date-time picker to set the exact kick-off moment
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the form">
    Click **+ Añadir**. The form sends `POST /api/admin/partidos` with `rival`, `fecha`, `ubicacion`, `jugado: false`, and scores initialised to `0 – 0`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the fixture appears">
    The table refreshes and the new fixture appears in date order. The match is marked as not yet played.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recording a match result

After a match has taken place, update the row for that fixture directly in the table.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the match">
    Locate the fixture in the Partidos table by scrolling to its date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the score">
    In the **Goles L/V** column, type the local (home) goals in the left input and the visiting (away) goals in the right input.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the result">
    Click **Guardar** in the row. This sends `PUT /api/admin/partidos/:id` with the updated scores and sets `jugado = true`, marking the match as played. A confirmation message appears and the table refreshes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Clicking **Guardar** always sets `jugado = true` regardless of whether you changed the score or only updated other fields. This is the intended behaviour — saving any change to a match marks it as having been played.
</Note>

## Editing match details

You can update the rival name, date, or location for any match directly in the table without having to delete and recreate the entry.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Edit the fields in the row">
    Click into the **Rival**, **Fecha y Hora**, or **Ubicación** field for the match you want to change and type the new value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Guardar**. All fields in the row — rival, date, location, and scores — are submitted together in a single update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you need to reschedule a match without recording a result yet, clear the score fields back to `0` before saving to avoid inadvertently logging a 0–0 result as played.
</Tip>

## Deleting a match

Click **Borrar** in the row of the match you want to remove. Confirm the prompt to send `DELETE /api/admin/partidos/:id`. The match is permanently removed and the table refreshes.

<Warning>
  Deleting a match cannot be undone. If you only need to reschedule, edit the date field instead of deleting the entry.
</Warning>

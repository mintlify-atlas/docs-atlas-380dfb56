The store is managed across two tabs in the admin dashboard: **Tienda** handles the product catalogue and **Ventas** handles customer orders. Together they give you a complete view of what is on sale, how much stock remains, and which orders need to be fulfilled.

## The Tienda tab

Open the admin dashboard at `/admin` and click the **Tienda** tab. The page shows a form to add new products at the top and an **Inventario** table below listing every product currently in the store.

### Adding a product

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in the product form">
    Complete all required fields in the **Añadir Nuevo Producto** form:

    - **Nombre** — the product name displayed in the store
    - **Precio (€)** — the selling price; accepts decimals (e.g. `24.99`)
    - **Stock** — the initial quantity available
    - **Categoría** — a label used to group products (e.g. `Camisetas`, `Accesorios`)
    - **URL Imagen** — a full URL pointing to the product image; if left empty a placeholder image is used
    - **Descripción** — a short description shown on the product page
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the product">
    Click **PUBLICAR PRODUCTO**. The form sends `POST /api/admin/productos` with all six fields. The table refreshes and the new product appears immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Editing a product

Every product row in the inventory table is editable inline. You can change the name, category, image URL, description, price, and stock at any time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate the product">
    Find the product in the **Gestionar Inventario** table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the fields">
    Click into any editable field — name, category, image URL, description, price, or stock — and type the new value.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the changes">
    Click **Guardar** in the product row. This sends `PUT /api/admin/productos/:id` with all six fields. A confirmation message appears on success.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Stock warnings

Products with fewer than **5 units** in stock are highlighted in the table: the stock field turns red and a **¡Reponer!** label appears below it. These same items are counted in the `stockBajo` metric visible in the Ventas tab stats cards.

<Warning>
  Stock is decremented automatically when a customer completes a purchase. A customer buying 3 units of a product with 4 in stock will leave only 1 remaining. Monitor the stock column and replenish before it reaches zero to avoid blocking purchases.
</Warning>

### Removing a product

Click **Eliminar** in the product row. Confirm the prompt to send `DELETE /api/admin/productos/:id`. The product is permanently removed from the store and the table refreshes.

<Warning>
  Deleting a product is irreversible. If you want to temporarily hide a product from customers, reduce its stock to `0` instead of deleting it — this prevents purchases while keeping the product record intact.
</Warning>

## The Ventas tab

Click the **Ventas** tab to see the sales overview. Six metric cards appear at the top of the page followed by the full order table.

### Stats cards

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Recaudación Total | Total revenue across all orders in euros |
| Producto Estrella | Best-selling product by units sold |
| Stock Bajo | Number of products with fewer than 5 units remaining |
| Enviados | Count of orders with status `enviado` |
| Pendientes | Count of orders with status `pendiente` or no status |
| Total Histórico | Total number of orders ever placed |

### Viewing and filtering orders

The order table lists every transaction with the order ID, customer email, date, total amount, and current status.

- Use the **search field** to filter orders by customer email or order ID.
- Toggle **Mostrar: Solo Pendientes** to show only orders that still need to be dispatched.

### Updating order status

When a customer places an order it starts with the status `pendiente`. Once you have dispatched it, mark it as sent.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the pending order">
    Locate the order in the Ventas table. Orders with status `pendiente` show a **Marcar como Enviado** button in the Acción column.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as dispatched">
    Click **Marcar como Enviado**. This sends `PUT /api/admin/ventas/:id` with `estado: 'Enviado'`. A confirmation message appears and the order status updates in the table.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Once an order is marked as `enviado` the action button disappears and is replaced with the text "Completado". Orders cannot be moved back to `pendiente` through the UI.
</Tip>

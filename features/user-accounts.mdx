An FC Cañaveral account lets you complete purchases in the store and review every order you have placed. Registration takes a few seconds, and your session is maintained automatically between visits.

## Creating an account

Go to `/registro` to sign up. Fill in the three required fields and submit the form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your username">
    Choose a unique display name. This is the name shown on your profile and in confirmation messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Provide a valid email address. Each email can only be registered once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a password">
    Choose a password for your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the form">
    Click **REGISTRARSE**. Once registration is successful, you are redirected to `/login` to sign in with your new credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If the email address you enter is already registered, you will see an error message and can try a different address or sign in at `/login` instead.
</Note>

## Signing in

Navigate to `/login` and enter your email address and password, then click **ENTRAR**.

- **Regular users** are redirected to the home page (`/`) after a successful login.
- **Admin users** are redirected to `/admin` after a successful login.

Your session is stored in the browser. If your session expires while you are using the site, you will be notified and redirected to `/login` to authenticate again.

<Info>
Sessions are time-limited. You will need to sign in again after the session expires. There is no "remember me" option — this is by design to keep accounts secure.
</Info>

If you do not have an account yet, the login page includes a link to `/registro`.

## Account roles

Every account has one of two roles:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="user" icon="user">
    The default role for all newly registered accounts. Can browse the site, place orders, and view order history.
  </Card>
  <Card title="admin" icon="shield">
    Elevated access. Admin accounts are redirected to the `/admin` dashboard after login and have access to site management tools not available to regular users.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Profile and order history

Visit `/perfil` when signed in to view your complete purchase history. The profile page lists all orders associated with your account in a table with the following columns:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| ID | Unique order number prefixed with `#` |
| Fecha | Date the order was placed |
| Total | Order total in euros |
| Estado | Current order status |

### Order status

Orders can have one of three statuses:

- **pendiente** — order received and awaiting processing
- **pagado** — payment confirmed
- **enviado** — order dispatched

### Viewing order details

Click **Ver productos** on any order row to expand an itemised breakdown. Each line shows:

- Product name
- Quantity purchased
- Unit price at the time of purchase

Click **Ocultar** to collapse the details for that order.

<Tip>
If you have not placed any orders yet, the profile page displays a message indicating that your purchase history is empty. Visit `/tienda` to browse available merchandise.
</Tip>

## Signing out

You can sign out at any time from the navigation bar. Signing out clears your session and cart data from the browser immediately.
